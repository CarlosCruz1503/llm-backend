# The Garden Table - Payments, Tipping and Gift Cards

## Payment methods in the restaurant

Both locations accept cash, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Checks are not accepted.

## Splitting the bill

We can split a bill up to six ways per table, by item or in equal parts. Please tell your server before ordering if you want separate bills.

## Service charge and tipping

An 18% service charge is added automatically to bills for groups of eight or more. For groups of up to seven, tipping is optional and at your discretion. All tips are shared among the whole team, including the kitchen.

## Gift cards

- Gift cards are available in amounts of $25, $50 and $100, or any custom amount from $10 to $500.
- They can be bought at either location or on our website, as a physical card or by email.
- Gift cards are valid for three years from the date of purchase.
- Gift cards can be used at both locations, for dine-in bills, and for delivery and pickup orders placed on our website.
- Gift cards cannot be used on third-party delivery apps.
- Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- Lost gift cards can be replaced if you have the purchase receipt or the email.

## Corporate gift cards

Companies buying 20 or more gift cards receive a 10% bonus value. Contact the events team.

## Refunds

If you were charged incorrectly, contact us within 30 days with your receipt.

## Deposits

Deposits for private events are paid by card or bank transfer; see Events and Private Dining.

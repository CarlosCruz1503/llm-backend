# The Garden Table Downtown - Location, Parking and Transport

## Address

The Garden Table Downtown is at 214 Alder Street, in the Old Town district, on the corner of Alder Street and 3rd Avenue. This is our original location, open since 2019.

## Parking

The restaurant does not have its own parking lot. Options nearby:

- Street parking on Alder Street and 3rd Avenue. Meters are in operation from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Parking is free on Sundays and after 7:00 PM.
- Alder Street Garage, two blocks north on 5th Avenue. Dine-in guests receive up to two hours of free parking validation; bring your garage ticket to your server.

There is no valet service.

## Public transport

- Tram line 4 stops at "Alder and 3rd", directly in front of the restaurant.
- Bus lines 8 and 20 stop on 4th Avenue, a two-minute walk.

## Bicycles

There are bike racks for eight bicycles in front of the patio.

## Taxis and ride sharing

Pick-up and drop-off are easiest on 3rd Avenue, where there is a short loading zone.

## Finding the entrance

The main entrance is on Alder Street. The step-free entrance is on the 3rd Avenue side, next to the patio gate; see the Accessibility information for details.

## Neighborhood

Old Town is a busy area on Friday and Saturday evenings. If you are driving, allow extra time to find parking, or use the Alder Street Garage, which rarely fills before 8:00 PM.

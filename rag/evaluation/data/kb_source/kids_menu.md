# The Garden Table - Kids Menu

The kids menu is for children 12 years old and under. It is available during all service hours at both locations, including weekend brunch at Downtown. Each kids meal includes a small drink (apple juice, milk or lemonade) and a scoop of vanilla ice cream or fruit sorbet.

## Kids meals

- Mini mushroom pasta - $7. A smaller portion of our mushroom pasta, with or without mushrooms. Vegetarian.
- Chicken strips - $8. Oven-baked chicken strips with fries or carrot sticks.
- Veggie quesadilla - $7. Cheese and bean quesadilla with mild salsa. Vegetarian.
- Kids rice bowl - $7. Rice, steamed vegetables and grilled chicken or tofu.

## For families

- High chairs and booster seats are available at both locations; please mention them when you reserve.
- Crayons and a coloring placemat are offered to every child.
- The Riverside location has a small play corner near the family tables.
- Baby changing tables are in the accessible restrooms at both locations.

## Allergies and kids

The kids meals follow the same recipes as the adult dishes they are based on. The chicken strips are coated in a gluten-free crumb but are cooked in the same oven as dishes that contain gluten. Please tell your server about any allergy so the kitchen can confirm what is safe.

## Birthdays

Children celebrating a birthday receive a free scoop of ice cream with a candle. Families who want to bring a birthday cake should check the cake policy in the Restaurant Policies, since a per-person cake fee applies.

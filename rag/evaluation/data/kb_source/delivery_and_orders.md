# The Garden Table - Delivery and Online Orders

## Ordering

Orders for delivery or pickup are placed on our website. The online menu shows the regular menu (and the winter menu in season). Brunch dishes are not available for delivery or pickup.

## Delivery

- Delivery is made only from the Downtown location. Riverside offers pickup only.
- Delivery area: within 8 kilometers of the Downtown restaurant.
- Minimum order for delivery: $15.
- Delivery fee: $4. Delivery is free for orders over $40.
- Estimated delivery time: 35 to 50 minutes, longer on Friday and Saturday evenings.
- Delivery hours: from opening until 45 minutes before closing.

## Pickup

Pickup is available at both locations. Orders are usually ready in 20 minutes. Pickup orders can be paid online or in person, including cash at the counter.

## Payment for online orders

Online orders accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Gift cards and loyalty rewards can be redeemed on our website.

## Third-party delivery apps

The Downtown restaurant is also listed on two third-party delivery apps. Prices on those apps are set by the apps and may be higher. Gift cards, loyalty points, loyalty rewards and restaurant promotions cannot be used on third-party apps, and problems with those orders must be reported to the app.

## Packaging

Delivery and pickup orders are packed in compostable containers. Guests who join the reusable container program pay a $1 deposit per container, refunded when the container is returned to either location.

## Problems with an order

If something is missing or wrong in a website order, contact us within 24 hours with your order number and we will refund or replace the item.

## Large orders

For orders for 20 or more people, use our catering service instead of regular delivery.

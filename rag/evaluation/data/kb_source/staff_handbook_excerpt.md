# The Garden Table - Staff Handbook (Excerpt for Employees)

INTERNAL DOCUMENT FOR EMPLOYEES. This excerpt describes rules for staff working at The Garden Table. It does not describe rules for guests.

## Uniform and dress code for staff

- Front-of-house staff wear black trousers or a black skirt, a plain white or black shirt and the green Garden Table apron.
- Kitchen staff wear the chef jacket provided by the restaurant, black trousers and a hat or hairnet.
- All staff must wear closed, non-slip shoes.
- Jewelry is limited to small earrings and a plain ring for kitchen staff.
- Name badges must be worn during service.

## Shifts

- Shifts are published every Thursday for the following week.
- Shift swaps must be approved by a manager.
- Staff should arrive 15 minutes before the start of each shift for the pre-service briefing.

## Staff meals

A free staff meal is served before each evening shift. Staff receive a 30% discount when dining at either location on their days off.

## Tips

Tips and the service charge are pooled and shared among all front-of-house and kitchen staff, according to hours worked.

## Allergens

All staff must complete allergen training in their first week. Servers must always pass allergy information to the kitchen in writing on the order.

## Health and safety

- Report any injury, however small, to the shift manager.
- Knives are carried pointing down and never left in sinks.
- The first aid kit is in the office next to the kitchen.

## Guest complaints

Staff should listen, apologize and inform the shift manager. Managers may offer to remake a dish or remove it from the bill.

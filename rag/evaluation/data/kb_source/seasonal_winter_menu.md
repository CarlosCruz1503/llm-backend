# The Garden Table - Winter Seasonal Menu

The winter menu runs every year from December 1 to February 28 at both locations. During this period the dishes below are added to the regular menu, and where a dish appears on both menus, the winter version and winter price replace the regular one. Outside the winter season these prices do not apply.

## Winter main courses

- Grilled salmon (winter edition) - $24. Salmon fillet with brown butter, roasted winter root vegetables and parsnip puree. Replaces the regular grilled salmon during the winter season.
- Beef and barley stew - $19. Slow-cooked beef shoulder, pearl barley, carrots and thyme, served with sourdough.
- Pumpkin risotto - $17. Roasted pumpkin, sage and parmesan. Vegetarian.
- Lentil and squash pie - $16. Puff pastry pie with spiced lentils and butternut squash. Vegetarian.

## Winter starters

- Roasted chestnut soup - $8. Vegetarian.
- Warm beet salad - $9. Roasted beets, goat cheese and walnuts. Vegetarian. Contains tree nuts.

## Winter desserts

- Spiced apple crumble - $8. Baked apples with an oat crumble and vanilla custard.
- Hot chocolate pot - $7. Dark chocolate cream with whipped cream.

## Winter drinks

- Mulled wine - $9. Red wine warmed with orange and spices.
- Hot apple cider (non-alcoholic) - $5.

## Why a winter menu

Our suppliers' harvest changes with the season, and the winter menu lets the kitchen use root vegetables, squash and stored apples while they are at their best. Some summer ingredients, such as fresh tomatoes and soft herbs, become expensive or must be shipped from far away in winter, so dishes that depend on them are reduced or adjusted.

The winter salmon costs more than the regular salmon because winter supply is smaller and the dish includes additional sides. The regular price of $21 returns on March 1.

The Allergen Guide covers the regular menu. For allergen information on winter dishes, please ask your server, who can check the recipe card with the kitchen.

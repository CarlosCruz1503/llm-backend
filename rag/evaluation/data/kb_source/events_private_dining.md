# The Garden Table - Events and Private Dining

All private events are hosted at the Downtown location. Riverside does not have a private room.

## The Greenhouse Room

Our private dining room, The Greenhouse Room, is a glass-roofed room on the ground floor overlooking the patio.

- Capacity: up to 24 guests seated, or up to 35 guests for a standing reception.
- Equipment: screen, projector, microphone and Wi-Fi, included at no extra charge.
- Minimum spend: $400 from Sunday to Thursday, and $600 on Friday and Saturday. The minimum spend covers food and drinks; if the bill is lower, the difference is charged as a room fee.
- Deposit: 25% of the minimum spend, paid when booking. The deposit is refundable if the event is cancelled at least 7 days in advance.

## Group menus

Private events use fixed group menus, chosen at least 5 days before the event:

- Three-course menu - $45 per person (starter, main course, dessert).
- Four-course menu - $58 per person (two starters, main course, dessert).
- Vegetarian and vegan alternatives are available for every course if requested in advance.
- Drinks packages start at $25 per person for two hours.

## Full restaurant buyout

For groups larger than the Greenhouse Room, the whole Downtown restaurant can be booked for 25 to 90 guests seated. The minimum spend for a buyout is $4,500 on Friday and Saturday and $3,000 from Sunday to Thursday. Buyouts must be booked at least two weeks in advance.

## Events outside the restaurant

For events at your office or home, see our Catering service.

## Booking

Event requests are handled by the events team by email. The 18% service charge for groups of eight or more applies to all private events.

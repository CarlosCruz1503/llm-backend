# The Garden Table - Weekend Brunch

Weekend brunch is served on Saturdays and Sundays from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM at the Downtown location only. The Riverside location does not serve brunch. After 2:00 PM the regular all-day menu is served.

Brunch reservations are recommended, especially on Sundays. Walk-in guests are seated in order of arrival. The kids menu is also available during brunch.

## Brunch plates

- Shakshuka - $13. Eggs baked in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce, with feta and sourdough. Vegetarian.
- Buttermilk pancakes - $11. Three pancakes with maple syrup, berries and whipped butter. Vegetarian.
- Avocado toast - $10. Smashed avocado, chili flakes, lime and seeds on sourdough. Vegan.
- Garden benedict - $14. Poached eggs, sauteed spinach and hollandaise on an English muffin. Vegetarian.
- Smoked salmon benedict - $16. Poached eggs, smoked salmon and hollandaise.
- Brunch burger - $16. Beef patty, fried egg, bacon and cheddar on a brioche bun, with fries.
- Granola bowl - $9. House granola, yogurt and seasonal fruit. Vegetarian. Contains tree nuts.

## Good to know

Brunch dishes are cooked to order and the kitchen gets busy after 11:30 AM, so expect longer waits at peak time. Bottomless coffee refills are available while you are seated. Brunch plates cannot be ordered for delivery or pickup; the website ordering menu only offers the regular menu.

Loyalty points can be earned on brunch bills in the same way as on any other dine-in bill.

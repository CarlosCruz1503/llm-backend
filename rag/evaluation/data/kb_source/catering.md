# The Garden Table - Catering

Our catering service brings The Garden Table to your office, home or venue. Catering is prepared in the Downtown kitchen.

## Minimums and notice

- Minimum order: 20 guests.
- Orders must be placed at least 72 hours in advance. For 75 guests or more, we need at least 5 days of notice.
- Catering is delivered within 15 kilometers of the Downtown restaurant. Delivery and setup are free for orders over $600; otherwise a $40 delivery fee applies.

## Catering menus

- Garden buffet - $28 per person. Two salads, two main courses (one vegetarian), bread and one dessert.
- Boxed lunches - $16 per person. A sandwich or grain bowl, a side salad, fruit and a cookie.
- Canapes reception - $22 per person. Eight pieces per person, including at least three vegetarian options.
- Breakfast platters - $14 per person. Pastries, fruit, yogurt and granola, and coffee.

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options can be included in any menu. Allergen labels are attached to every tray.

## Staff and equipment

- Service staff can be booked at $35 per hour per server, with a minimum of three hours.
- Disposable plates and cutlery are compostable and included.
- Reusable serving equipment (chafing dishes, platters) can be rented for $50 per order and is collected the next working day.

## Payment

A 50% deposit is required to confirm the order. The balance is paid on delivery or by invoice for corporate clients.

## Cancellations

Orders cancelled more than 48 hours before delivery are refunded in full. Orders cancelled within 48 hours are charged 50% of the total.

## Not sure what you need?

For seated dinners in a private space at the restaurant, see Events and Private Dining. For smaller orders under 20 people, use regular delivery or pickup.

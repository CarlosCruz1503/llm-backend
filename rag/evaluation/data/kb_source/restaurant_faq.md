# The Garden Table - Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended for dinner and weekend brunch but not required. Walk-in guests are welcome when tables are free. Groups of eight or more must reserve at least 24 hours in advance.

## Can I make a reservation for a large group?

Yes. Groups of eight or more should reserve at least 24 hours in advance. For more than 24 guests, see Events and Private Dining.

## Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. Several dishes are vegetarian, and the vegetable curry, garden salad, hummus plate and fruit sorbet are vegan. The garden burger can be made vegan on request.

## Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome on the Downtown patio. Dogs are not allowed on the Riverside terrace. Service animals are welcome everywhere.

## Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for guests at both locations. The password is printed on your receipt.

## Can I bring a birthday cake?

Yes. A cake fee of $3 per person applies.

## Is there parking?

Downtown has no private lot; dine-in guests get up to two hours of free validation at the Alder Street Garage. Riverside has a free parking lot.

## Do you deliver?

Yes, from Downtown, within 8 kilometers. Riverside offers pickup only.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, at both locations and on our website.

## Are children welcome?

Yes. We have a kids menu for children 12 and under, high chairs and booster seats.

## Do you add a service charge?

An 18% service charge is added to bills for groups of eight or more. For smaller groups, tipping is at your discretion.

## Can I order food to take away?

Yes. Pickup orders can be placed on our website for both locations.

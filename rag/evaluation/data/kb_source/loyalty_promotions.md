# The Garden Table - Garden Club Loyalty Program and Promotions

## Joining

The Garden Club is free. Guests can join on our website or ask their server. Members are identified by phone number.

## Earning points

- Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent, before tax and service charge.
- Points are earned on dine-in bills at both locations and on delivery and pickup orders placed on our website.
- Points are not earned on third-party delivery apps, on gift card purchases or on catering orders.
- On Tuesdays, members earn double points on dine-in bills.

## Rewards

- Every 100 points can be exchanged for a $10 reward.
- Only one reward can be used per bill.
- Rewards can be used at both locations and on website orders, but not on third-party apps.
- Points expire after 12 months without any activity on the account.

## Birthday reward

During their birthday month, members receive a free dessert of their choice from the regular menu, once per year, with any dine-in main course. The birthday dessert can be combined with a points reward on the same bill.

## Current promotions

- Tuesday double points, as described above.
- Lunch combo, Tuesday to Friday from 11:30 AM to 3:00 PM: any starter and any main course for $19, at both locations.
- Student discount: 10% off dine-in bills from Monday to Thursday with a valid student card, at the Riverside location only.

Promotions cannot be combined with each other, but members still earn points on promotional bills. Promotions do not apply on third-party delivery apps.

## Leaving the program

Members can close their account at any time by contacting us. Unused points are cancelled when the account is closed.

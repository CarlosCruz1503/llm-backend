# The Garden Table - Allergen Guide

This guide lists the main allergens in the standard recipes of the regular menu. Our kitchen handles gluten, dairy, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame and soy. Even when a dish does not contain an allergen, cross-contact is possible because ingredients share the same kitchen. Always tell your server about allergies before ordering.

## Starters

- Roasted tomato soup: gluten (served with sourdough; can be served without bread).
- Garden salad: no major allergens. The vinaigrette contains mustard.
- Hummus plate: sesame (tahini), gluten (flatbread).
- Crispy calamari: shellfish and mollusc (squid), gluten, egg (aioli). Fried in the shared fryer.

## Main courses

- Mushroom pasta: gluten, dairy (parmesan and cream sauce), egg (fresh pasta).
- Vegetable curry: no gluten, no dairy. Contains coconut. Gluten-free and vegan.
- Garden burger: gluten (bun), dairy (cheddar), egg (herb mayo). Fries are cooked in the shared fryer.
- Grilled chicken bowl: no gluten. Herb sauce contains dairy (yogurt).
- Grilled salmon: fish, dairy (lemon butter). Gluten-free.
- Steak frites: dairy (garlic butter). Fries are cooked in the shared fryer.

## Sides

- Fries: no gluten ingredients, but they are cooked in the same fryer as the calamari, so they are not safe for guests with celiac disease.
- Green beans with almonds: tree nuts.

## Desserts

- Lemon cheesecake: gluten (biscuit base), dairy, egg. No nuts in the recipe.
- Chocolate almond torte: tree nuts (almonds), egg, dairy. Gluten-free.
- Vanilla ice cream: dairy, egg. No nuts in the recipe.
- Seasonal fruit sorbet: no major allergens. Vegan and gluten-free.

## Nut allergies

The only regular-menu items that contain nuts are the chocolate almond torte and the green beans with almonds. Desserts without nuts in the recipe are the lemon cheesecake, the vanilla ice cream and the fruit sorbet, but they are prepared in a pastry area that also handles almonds, so we cannot guarantee they are free from traces of nuts.

## Celiac disease and gluten

Gluten-free main courses are the vegetable curry, the grilled chicken bowl and the grilled salmon. Ask for them without fries. We do not have a dedicated gluten-free fryer.

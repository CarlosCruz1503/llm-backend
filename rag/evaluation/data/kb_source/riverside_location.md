# The Garden Table Riverside - Guest Information

The Garden Table Riverside is our second location. It opened in March 2026 at 88 Quay Road, in the Riverside district, next to the river walk. Riverside serves the same regular menu, winter menu and kids menu as Downtown. This sheet explains what is different at Riverside.

## Opening hours (Riverside)

- Monday to Thursday: 12:00 PM to 10:00 PM
- Friday and Saturday: 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM
- Sunday: 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM

Riverside is open on Mondays, unlike Downtown.

## What Riverside does not offer

- No weekend brunch. Brunch is served only at Downtown.
- No happy hour.
- No private dining room. Private events are hosted at Downtown.
- No delivery. Riverside offers pickup orders only.

## Seating

Riverside has 80 indoor seats and a covered river terrace with 30 seats, open all year. Because the terrace is next to the public river walk, dogs are not allowed on the Riverside terrace; only service animals are allowed at Riverside.

## Parking and transport

Riverside has its own free parking lot with 60 spaces, including four accessible spaces. The Quay Road bus stop (lines 12 and 15) is one minute away on foot.

## Reservations

Reservations for Riverside are taken by phone only. The same rules apply as Downtown: tables are held for 15 minutes, and groups of eight or more must reserve 24 hours in advance.

## Families

Riverside has a small play corner near the family tables and two baby changing tables.

## Accessibility

Riverside is on a single floor with step-free access from the parking lot, wide aisles and an accessible restroom.

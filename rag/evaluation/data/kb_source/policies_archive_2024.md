# The Garden Table - Restaurant Policies 2024 (ARCHIVED)

ARCHIVED DOCUMENT. These policies were in effect from January 2024 until December 31, 2025. They have been superseded by the Restaurant Policies effective January 1, 2026, and are kept only for reference. Do not use this document to answer questions about current policies.

## Opening hours (2024)

- Monday: closed
- Tuesday to Sunday: 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM

There was no weekend brunch service in 2024.

## Reservations (2024)

Reservations were taken by phone only. Tables were held for 10 minutes after the reservation time. Groups of ten or more had to reserve 48 hours in advance. There was no cancellation fee.

## Pets (2024)

Pets were not allowed anywhere in the restaurant, including the patio. Service animals were welcome.

## Outdoor seating (2024)

The patio had 24 seats and was open from May to September.

## Bringing your own wine (2024)

Guests could bring their own wine. The corkage fee was $15 per bottle, with no limit on the number of bottles.

## Outside food and cakes (2024)

Guests could bring a celebration cake free of charge. Other outside food was not allowed.

## Payments (2024)

Cash and major credit cards were accepted. Mobile wallets were not accepted.

## Why the policies changed

In 2025 the restaurant expanded the patio, added weekend brunch and moved reservations online. The 2026 policies reflect these changes and add a cancellation fee for large groups, because late cancellations of large bookings left many tables empty on busy nights.

# The Garden Table - Accessibility

We want every guest to be comfortable at both of our locations. If you have any access needs, mention them when you reserve and we will prepare your table.

## Downtown (214 Alder Street)

- Step-free entrance on the 3rd Avenue side, next to the patio gate. The main Alder Street entrance has two steps.
- The indoor dining room and the outdoor patio are on the same level as the step-free entrance. A short ramp connects the dining room to the patio.
- One accessible restroom on the ground floor, with grab bars and a baby changing table.
- The private dining room (The Greenhouse Room) is on the ground floor and is step-free.
- Tables can be removed or rearranged to make room for wheelchairs.

## Riverside (88 Quay Road)

- Single floor, step-free from the parking lot and from the river walk.
- Four accessible parking spaces in our own lot.
- Accessible restroom with grab bars.

## Menus

- Large-print menus are available at both locations.
- The full menu and allergen guide can be read on our website with screen readers.
- Staff can read the menu aloud and describe dishes on request.

## Service animals

Service animals are welcome in all areas of both locations, including the indoor dining rooms and the Riverside terrace, where pets are otherwise not allowed.

## Hearing and communication

The Downtown dining room has sound-absorbing panels; for a quieter table, ask for the back section away from the bar. Reservations can be made online for guests who prefer not to use the phone (Downtown only; Riverside takes reservations by phone).

## Sensory-friendly hours

On Tuesdays from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM the Downtown dining room plays no music and keeps lighting low. This time is popular with families of children with sensory sensitivities.

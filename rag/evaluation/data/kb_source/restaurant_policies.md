# The Garden Table - Restaurant Policies (Downtown)

Effective January 1, 2026. These policies replace all earlier versions, including the 2024 policies, and apply to the Downtown location at 214 Alder Street. The Riverside location has its own guest information sheet.

## Opening hours

- Monday: closed
- Tuesday to Thursday: 11:30 AM to 9:30 PM
- Friday: 11:30 AM to 10:30 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM (brunch from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM)
- Sunday: 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM (brunch from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM)

The kitchen takes last orders 30 minutes before closing.

## Reservations

Reservations can be made online or by phone. Walk-in guests are welcome when tables are free. Tables are held for 15 minutes after the reservation time. Groups of eight or more must reserve at least 24 hours in advance. For groups of eight or more, cancellations made less than 24 hours before the reservation are charged $10 per person.

## Pets

Dogs are welcome on the outdoor patio and must be kept on a leash. Dogs are not allowed in the indoor dining room. Service animals are welcome everywhere in the restaurant.

## Outdoor seating

The outdoor patio has 40 seats and is open from April to October, weather permitting. Guests can request a patio table when making a reservation; patio requests are not guaranteed.

## Bringing your own wine

Guests may bring their own wine. The corkage fee is $20 per 750 ml bottle, with a maximum of two bottles per table. Corkage is not available for spirits.

## Outside food and cakes

Outside food is not allowed, with one exception: guests may bring a celebration cake. A cake fee of $3 per person is charged, which covers plating, candles and serving.

## Children

Children are welcome at all times. High chairs are available.

## Lost and found

Items left at the restaurant are kept for 30 days.

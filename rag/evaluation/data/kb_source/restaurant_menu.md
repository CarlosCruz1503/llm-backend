# The Garden Table - Restaurant Menu

The Garden Table is a casual restaurant focused on seasonal food, homemade sauces and locally sourced ingredients. This is the regular all-day menu, served at both the Downtown and the Riverside locations. During the winter season (December 1 to February 28) some dishes are replaced or repriced; see the separate Winter Seasonal Menu.

All prices are in US dollars and do not include the service charge that applies to groups of eight or more.

## Starters

- Roasted tomato soup - $7. Slow-roasted tomatoes, basil oil and toasted sourdough. Vegetarian.
- Garden salad - $8. Mixed greens, cucumber, tomato, carrots and house vinaigrette. Vegan.
- Hummus plate - $9. Chickpea hummus with tahini, olive oil, pickled vegetables and warm flatbread. Vegan.
- Crispy calamari - $11. Lightly battered squid with lemon aioli.

## Main courses

- Mushroom pasta - $16. Fresh tagliatelle with roasted mushrooms, parmesan and a light cream sauce. Vegetarian.
- Vegetable curry - $15. Seasonal vegetables and chickpeas in a coconut and tomato curry, served with jasmine rice. Vegan.
- Garden burger - $15. Black bean and beet patty, cheddar, pickles and herb mayo on a brioche bun, with fries. Vegetarian. A vegan version (vegan bun, no cheese, no mayo) is available on request.
- Grilled chicken bowl - $17. Grilled chicken, roasted vegetables, rice and herb sauce.
- Grilled salmon - $21. Salmon fillet with lemon butter, crushed potatoes and green beans.
- Steak frites - $26. Grass-fed sirloin with garlic butter, fries and watercress. Our most expensive main course.

## Sides

- Fries - $5
- Green beans with almonds - $5
- Jasmine rice - $3

## Desserts

- Lemon cheesecake - $8. Baked cheesecake on a biscuit base with lemon curd.
- Chocolate almond torte - $9. Flourless chocolate cake made with ground almonds.
- Vanilla ice cream - $6. Two scoops, made in house.
- Seasonal fruit sorbet - $6. Vegan.

## Notes

Vegetarian main courses are the mushroom pasta, the vegetable curry and the garden burger. The only main course that is vegan as served is the vegetable curry; the garden burger can be made vegan on request.

Dietary labels describe the standard recipe. Guests with allergies should read the Allergen Guide and tell their server before ordering. Portion sizes are designed for one person; starters can be shared.

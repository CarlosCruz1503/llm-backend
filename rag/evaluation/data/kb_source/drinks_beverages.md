# The Garden Table - Drinks and Beverages

## Non-alcoholic drinks

- House lemonade - $4
- Iced tea - $4
- Sparkling water (bottle) - $3
- Filtered still water - free
- Coffee - $3
- Espresso drinks (latte, cappuccino, flat white) - $4.50
- Herbal tea - $3
- Garden cooler (cucumber, mint and lime soda) - $5

Oat milk and almond milk are available for coffee at no extra charge.

## Cocktails

- Garden spritz - $12. Aperitivo, prosecco, soda and orange.
- Basil gin smash - $13. Gin, basil, lemon and sugar.
- Classic margarita - $12.
- Espresso martini - $13.

## Beer

- Local craft lager or IPA (draft) - $7
- Non-alcoholic beer - $5

## Wine

Wine by the glass costs $9 to $12. Bottles range from $34 to $60. The list changes every season and focuses on small producers. Ask your server for the current list.

## Happy hour

Happy hour runs Tuesday to Friday from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM at the Downtown location, at the bar and on the patio. During happy hour cocktails are $2 off and draft beer is $5. Happy hour prices do not apply to bottled wine. Happy hour is not offered at the Riverside location.

## Bringing your own wine

Guests may bring their own wine. A corkage fee applies; see the Restaurant Policies for the current amount and limits.

## Responsible service

We serve alcohol only to guests aged 21 and over and may ask for identification. Staff may refuse service to guests who appear intoxicated.

# The Garden Table - Service and Guest Experience

## Waiting times

On Friday and Saturday evenings the waiting time for walk-in guests at Downtown is usually 20 to 30 minutes. Walk-in guests can join a text-message waitlist and wait nearby; we send a message when the table is ready. During weekday lunch, most walk-in guests are seated right away.

## Seating requests

We try to honor requests for a quiet table, a booth, the patio or a window table, but we cannot guarantee them. Mention them when you reserve.

## If a dish is not right

If a dish is not as expected, tell your server. We will remake it or offer another dish. There is no charge for a dish you send back because of a problem with its quality.

## Water and bread

Filtered still water is free and refilled during the meal. Bread is served on request with the starters, free of charge.

## Allergies

Tell your server about any allergy before ordering. Servers pass allergy information to the kitchen in writing, and the kitchen confirms which dishes are suitable. See the Allergen Guide.

## Special occasions

Let us know if you are celebrating. We can add a candle to a dessert and write a short message on the plate. Guests can also bring a celebration cake; a cake fee applies, as explained in the Restaurant Policies.

## Feedback

Feedback can be left on the comment card included with the bill, on our website, or by email. Managers read all feedback every week and reply within three working days to messages that need an answer.

## Lost items

Ask any member of staff, or contact us by email. Items are kept for 30 days.

## Noise

Downtown can be lively on weekend evenings. For a quieter experience, choose an early dinner or a weekday lunch, or ask for a table in the back section away from the bar.
